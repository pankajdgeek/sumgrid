SumGrid is a free daily number puzzle that challenges your logic skills in minutes, not hours.

HOW TO PLAY
Fill every cell in the grid with a number so that each row and each column adds up to its target sum. Every puzzle has exactly one solution — no guessing required. Start with a 3×3 grid and work your way up to 5×5 as your skills grow.

THREE DIFFICULTY LEVELS
• Beginner (3×3) — Perfect for a quick brain warm-up. Solve in under 2 minutes.
• Easy (4×4) — A satisfying daily challenge. Most players finish in 3–5 minutes.
• Medium (5×5) — A true logic puzzle for number enthusiasts. Can you beat 10 minutes?

ONE NEW PUZZLE EVERY DAY
A fresh puzzle drops at midnight, and every player worldwide gets the same puzzle. Compare your solve time with friends by sharing your spoiler-free result card — no numbers revealed, just a grid of emoji showing which cells you solved.

BUILT FOR YOUR DAILY ROUTINE
SumGrid is designed to be a calm, focused moment in your day. No flashing ads. No pop-ups. No account required. Just open the app, solve the puzzle, and get on with your day. Build a streak by solving every day — earn badges at 7, 30, 100, and 365 days.

WHY PLAYERS LOVE SUMGRID
Unlike sudoku, SumGrid puzzles are compact and quick. Unlike crosswords, no trivia knowledge is needed — just pure number logic. It is the perfect brain teaser for commutes, coffee breaks, or winding down before bed.
